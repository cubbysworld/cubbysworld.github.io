Cubby's World: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version

  • We do not collect your information or your child's.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your phone and is never sent to us.
  • The app does not ask for permission to use the internet. It works offline.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Cubby's World ("the app", "we", "us") is a children's app of bedtime stories, learning and games. You can reach us at cubbysworldapp@gmail.com. We reply within 10 working days.

2. What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves some things in its own private storage on your phone. This data stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Child profiles you create: a name, an age and a picture (up to two profiles)
  • Progress in lessons, games and stories, favourite stories, stars, streaks, badges and certificates
  • Drawings, saved words and other things your child makes in the app
  • Family settings, Bedtime Mode and the sleep timer
  • The Parents Area PIN and two security answers, kept scrambled in your phone's secure storage, and a note of whether Premium is active

The app does not back this up to the cloud. If you uninstall the app, it is removed.

3. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number or a password. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We never receive anything your child does in the app.

4. Deleting information

  • Erase a child's data: Parents Area > Privacy center > Erase. This removes that child's progress, stars, badges, drawings and settings from the phone.
  • Remove a child profile: Parents Area > My Family. Removing a profile also erases everything saved for that child.
  • Everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

5. Children

Cubby's World is made for children, and a grown-up controls the settings. Purchases and settings are behind a Parents Area PIN. Because the app collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and there is nothing for a parent to review or ask us to delete. If you have a question about your child's privacy, write to us at the address above.

6. Purchases

Premium is an optional subscription bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, so we never see your card or bank details. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium is active, and keeps that answer on your phone. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases made through Google Play. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions).

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Google Play Billing: to buy and check Premium.
  • Network state: to know whether the phone is online, so the app can show a short note. Nothing is sent.

The app never uses the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts or your files. In "Read to Cubby" a grown-up listens and taps how it went. Nothing is recorded.

8. Voices

Story and word voices come from your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not send any text anywhere. If you have chosen a voice that your phone's speech engine processes online, that is handled by the engine you chose and is outside this app.

9. Links

The Privacy Policy, Terms of use, Rate, Share and Contact buttons open your browser, the Play Store or your email app, and only when a grown-up taps them.

10. Security

The Parents Area PIN is stored as a scrambled value, not as the PIN itself, and the security answers are kept in your phone's secure storage. The app asks for the PIN again after a short time away, and waits longer after several wrong tries.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works with information, we will update this page and the date at the top, and the app's Privacy & Safety page will say so.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your child's privacy: cubbysworldapp@gmail.com.
