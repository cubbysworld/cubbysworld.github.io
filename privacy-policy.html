Cubby's World: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

  • We do not collect your information or your child's.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your phone and is never sent to us.
  • The app does not ask for permission to use the internet. It works offline.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Cubby's World ("the app", "we", "us") is a children's app of bedtime stories, learning and games. You can reach us at cubbysworldapp@gmail.com.

2. What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves some things in its own private storage on your phone. This data stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Child profiles (for example a nickname and an avatar chosen by a parent)
  • Learning and game progress, stars, streaks, badges and certificates, favourites, saved words, and things your child makes (such as drawings, their world and Cubby's room)
  • Mood check-ins, level checks and reading practice results (in Read to Cubby a grown-up listens and taps how it went; nothing is recorded)
  • Friend or pet names a parent chooses to use inside stories
  • Settings, such as language, text size, bedtime mode, the sleep timer and screen-time limits
  • Screen-time totals shown to parents in the Parents Area
  • The parent PIN (kept as a scrambled value, not as the digits) and the two security answers used to reset it (kept encrypted)
  • Whether Premium is active, so the app can still work when the phone is offline

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, advertising IDs, device identifiers or usage analytics. The app never uses the microphone or the camera, and it takes no photos and makes no voice recordings. The app has no ads, no social features and no chat, and we never sell data.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Network state: only to know whether the phone is online, so the app can show a friendly "you're offline" note. Nothing is sent.
  • Google Play Billing: to let a parent buy or restore Premium through Google Play.

The app does not request the internet permission and does not request access to your storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google, not us, handles your payment, your Google account and your subscription records, under Google's Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium is active on your account. We never see your card details, your name or your email address. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play at any time.

6. Story and word narration

Narration uses the text-to-speech voice built into your phone. If you choose an online voice in your phone's settings, the provider of that voice (for example Google) may process the text being read, under its own privacy policy. This is a setting on your phone, not something the app sends.

7. If you contact us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write. Feedback emails started from the Parents Area are pre-filled with the app version, your Android version and your phone model to help us fix problems. You can read and delete these details before you send. We use your message only to reply and to improve the app, and we don't share it.

8. Links and websites

Some buttons open pages outside the app, such as this policy, our Terms of use, or Google Play. Those pages are on other websites, which may record your visit as any website does. This policy covers only the app.

9. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children, with a parent in charge. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not need accounts or parental consent forms. Settings, Subscription, purchases and the Parents Area are protected by a parent PIN, with a short lock after too many wrong tries, so a child cannot buy anything. We follow Google Play's Families Policy. The areas children use have no ads and no links to other apps or websites; the few links that exist (rating, sharing, email, this policy) are inside the Parents Area, behind the PIN.

10. Keeping data safe

  • Sensitive items (the PIN, the security answers and the Premium status) are stored encrypted using your phone's secure storage.
  • The app does not back up its data to Google Drive or any cloud.
  • Lessons, games, stories and the Parents Area cannot be captured by the phone's screenshot or screen recording tools.

11. Keeping and deleting data

The app's data stays on your phone until you remove it. To delete it, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Apps → Cubby's World → Storage → Clear data). Because we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side.

If you forget both your PIN and your security answers, the PIN cannot be reset and the app has to be reinstalled, which also removes everything saved on your phone. A Premium subscription is not lost, because it belongs to your Google Play account.

12. Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that need the internet, an account or analytics, we will update this policy first, tell you in the app, and ask a parent before anything is collected. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Write to cubbysworldapp@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 10 working days.

This is a plain-language policy for the app as it works today. Please have it checked for your country before you publish it.
